# 13 — Market and User Validation

**Status:** Template to be completed with real evidence. Nothing here is a finding until interviews and teardowns are done. Complete before Month 2.

## 1. Purpose

v1.0 defined what to build but not why customers would choose it. This document defines the research needed to establish a differentiator, a target segment and a pricing basis.

## 2. Competitor teardown

Analyse idChess, ChessZCam and any local or regional alternatives (including DGT-based workflows, Lichess Broadcast, manual PGN relay and spreadsheet-based tournament management). Use public information, demos and customer conversations only. Do not copy UI, assets, copy or trade dress.

| Dimension | What to record |
|---|---|
| Customer segment | Who they sell to, evidence from case studies |
| Hardware | Cameras/boards required, cost, mounting, setup time |
| Reliability | Public complaints, forum posts, organiser anecdotes |
| Spectator experience | Mobile quality, find-a-player, delay, PGN, embeds |
| Arbiter workflow | Correction flow, exception handling, setup effort |
| Pricing and packaging | Per event, per board, subscription, hardware rental |
| Integrations | Pairing software, federations, Lichess, OBS |
| Support | Hours, language, on-site availability |
| Weaknesses | Concrete, evidenced pain points |

## 3. Interview programme

Target 8-12 interviews: organisers, arbiters, school/club coordinators, parents of junior players, federation officials.

### Interview guide (discovery, not pitching)
1. Walk me through the last tournament you ran or attended, start to finish.
2. What took the most time or caused the most stress?
3. How do spectators and parents follow games today? What do they ask you?
4. What tools do you use for pairings, results and publishing? What do you export or retype?
5. Have you used live broadcast or camera/electronic boards? What happened?
6. What hardware do you already own?
7. What would you pay for, and what do you pay today?
8. What would make you unwilling to switch?
9. Would you place phones on tables as cameras? Whose phones, who charges and supervises them, and what worries you?
10. How would parents, players and the venue react to a camera over each board? What consent do you already collect?

### Record per interview
Segment, event size, tools used, top 3 pain points (verbatim), hardware owned, budget signal, willingness to pilot, consent status.

## 5. Validation gates
- **Gate A (Month 1 end):** at least 8 interviews, competitor teardown, positioning draft.
- **Gate B (Month 3):** a design partner has run an event using the manual workflow and given written feedback.
- **Gate C (Month 5):** at least one paid pilot or signed letter of intent; otherwise revisit segment and scope before building further.
